# Home — Natan / j0ld

## Scope and mode

- Surface: the single homepage and its six sections (Destaques, Perfis, Biblioteca, Desejos, Projetos, Alternativas).
- Mode: Experience. The collection itself leads; the interface recedes.
- Audience: friends and the gaming community arriving from one shared link, mostly on phones at night, some on desktop.
- Job: find a profile fast, browse the kept games, leave with one recommendation worth trying.
- Proof on hand: real profile URLs and handles, 18 library covers, 10 wishlist covers, 12 app icons, the hero video/poster, one shipped project (Calculadora Liquid Glass).
- Constraints: static HTML/CSS/ES modules, no build step, Vercel, pt-BR only. Preserve every link, handle and section. No fabricated claims.
- Standing preference: the site's own identity is the brand. No external reference; the incumbent dark navy / glass / serif world is the only bar, executed much better.

## Chosen direction

A craft-complete refinement of the incumbent world, not a replacement. Keep the night-navy ground, the glass surfaces where they do a job, the serif display voice and the calm Portuguese copy. Remove the decorative loader, eyebrows and section numbers. Tighten the vertical rhythm, make the profile grid and the library shelf the two strong moments, and give the page a real closing.

Memorable moment: the first viewport resolves immediately — video, name, one line, and a profile row that is obviously the fastest door — then the library shelf feels like a physical thing you can pull.

Unresolved: none. The project preview keeps the iframe with a graceful fallback; no new assets are required.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet personal corner that behaves like a well-made object: the visitor sees Natan, the profiles, and the kept games in one confident breath, and the page never makes them wait or hunt. It refuses the noisy gamer hub and the anonymous link-in-bio.

OWN-WORLD: Night-navy ground (#060b18 family), tinted glass only on the header, menu, profile cards and project preview; warm off-white text; a single pale-blue accent for actions and focus; cover art supplies the color. Instrument Serif for the name and section headings, Inter for UI and body, both with a clear scale step. No gradient text, no decorative eyebrow, no section numbers, no zero-offset glow.

STORY: The visitor understands "this is Natan, these are his corners, this is what he keeps installed, this is what he still wants, this is what he made, this is what he replaced." They open a profile, copy a handle when there is no URL, pull a game from the shelf, and leave with at least one recommendation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed hero video, darkened to a readable navy; the name "Natan" in the serif at display scale on the left; one supporting line; a single primary row of the six section links; immediately below it, a compact row of six profile shortcuts (Steam, Discord, Roblox, Instagram, Xbox, YouTube) as the first real action. The full profile section begins below the fold. No loader, no eyebrow, no scroll hint competing with the links.

FORM: Refinement of the incumbent world, pinned by the user's words "Só o site atual, mais bem acabado". seed: none (pinned). The craft bar is the current site, corrected: fewer decorative layers, stronger hierarchy, one authored motion moment, full keyboard and touch support.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
